Line 2 runs east–west across Toronto from Kipling Station in Etobicoke to Kennedy Station in Scarborough. It passes through several busy neighbourhoods, including High Park, Bloor West Village, Koreatown, and Greektown. The line intersects Line 1 at St. George and Bloor–Yonge, making it a major transfer corridor. It is known for consistent service and high ridership during peak hours.
