Line 1 is Toronto’s busiest subway line and forms a large U‑shape across the city. It begins at Vaughan Metropolitan Centre, travels south through York University, continues downtown through major hubs like St. George, Bloor–Yonge, and Union Station, and then heads north again toward Finch Station. The line connects major employment areas, universities, and transfer points to GO Transit and other TTC routes. Trains run frequently throughout the day, making it a core part of Toronto’s rapid transit network.
